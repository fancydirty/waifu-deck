# Waifu Deck icon brief

## Product signal

Waifu Deck is a fast, private anime-image discovery and collection app. The launcher icon should communicate anime character identity first; card/deck behavior is a secondary signature.

## Visual priorities

1. Recognizable anime mascot at 32-48 px.
2. One dominant face or eye and one memorable hair silhouette.
3. Original character design with no resemblance to an existing franchise.
4. Large flat color regions that survive Android launcher masks.
5. A subtle card-corner or four-point sparkle motif as the Waifu Deck signature.

## Palette

- Charcoal: `#252A33`
- Warm ivory: `#F7F8F6`
- Coral: `#FF5D57`
- Jade: `#29A875`

## Avoid

- Text, initials, or wordmarks inside the launcher icon.
- Full-body characters, scenery, clothing detail, and small props.
- Existing characters or provider images.
- Decorative gradients, glow, glossy 3D, and phone mockups.
- Essential detail outside the adaptive-icon safe area.

## Acceptance checks

- The identity remains readable at 48 px and 32 px.
- Circle, squircle, rounded-square, and square masks preserve the face and signature feature.
- The silhouette works in monochrome before an Android themed icon is produced.
- The final source can be simplified into controlled vector geometry.
